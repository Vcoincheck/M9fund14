# What is Midnight

### What Is Midnight – A Deep Dive

Midnight is a next-generation blockchain platform engineered for **rational privacy**—a paradigm that balances transparency with confidentiality. Shunning the all-or-nothing nature of traditional chains, Midnight empowers developers to selectively disclose data to the right parties while keeping sensitive information shielded.

At its technical core, Midnight employs **zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs)**—specifically **ZK-SNARKs**—to allow verifiable assertions without revealing underlying data. This ensures that only necessary information becomes public, vastly enhancing privacy for users and developers alike.

***

#### Key Pillars of Midnight

**1. Programmable Privacy**

Midnight’s smart contracts support both private and public states, enabling **selective disclosure**. Developers gain granular control over what data is shared and who can see it, helping maintain compliance with regulatory standards without compromising user confidentiality.

**2. Developer Experience & Compact Language**

Built with ease-of-use in mind, Midnight offers **Compact**, a TypeScript-based domain-specific language. This familiar environment accelerates dApp creation and reduces complexity, making privacy-enhanced development more accessible.

**3. Cooperative Tokenomics**

The network introduces a multi-token model—**NIGHT** for governance and **DUST** for transaction execution—and features a cross-chain "Glacier Drop" distribution strategy. This ensures inclusivity and encourages long-term engagement while promoting a multi-chain, collaborative ecosystem.

**4. Practical Use Cases**

Midnight’s programmable privacy unlocks real-world use cases across various domains, including:

* **Identity Verification**
* **Controlled Access to Tokenized Assets**
* **AI & Large Language Model (LLM) Applications**
* **Decentralized Credit Systems**

***

#### Why Midnight Matters

In today’s landscape, the transparent nature of blockchains can be a barrier—hindering mainstream adoption, exposing sensitive metadata, and failing to meet regulatory requirements. Midnight reimagines this by offering privacy that is **programmable, compliant, and scalable.**

Developers and enterprises can now build dApps that do not force a trade-off between privacy and utility. Whether designing DApps for finance, healthcare, identity, or AI workflows, Midnight ensures sensitive data is protected—only revealed when intended and in full compliance with rules.
